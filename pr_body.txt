📉 **The Stack Trace:**

(and similar panic for Stagger's `.evaluate()` fallback).

🧪 **Reproduction:** Run `cargo test -p anim-graph --test havoc_sequence_panic`. A sequence with `Sequence::new(vec![])` crashes the app immediately upon evaluation.
