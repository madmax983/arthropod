💣 Risk: Catastrophic Out-Of-Memory (OOM) panic prior to bounds checking when unpacking readback pixels with maliciously or mistakenly enormous width/height values.
🧪 Strategy: Moved the bounds check (`min_required_src_len`) up before the vector memory allocation (`try_reserve(total_size)`), allowing it to fail gracefully instead of crashing with OOM. The bounds check relies on simple padded multiplication constraints.
